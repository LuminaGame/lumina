SKM_Superhero_Female.glb
========================

The Third Person template character, built by
lumina/tool/build_third_person_content.dart from three packs by Quaternius
(https://quaternius.com), each released under CC0 1.0 Universal:

- Universal Base Characters [Standard]
  https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters
  Used: Base Characters/Godot - UE/Superhero_Female_FullBody.gltf and its
  textures (repacked as a GLB, textures downscaled and re-encoded as JPEG,
  unused vertex attributes removed, the three meshes joined into one).

- Universal Animation Library [Standard]
  https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library
  Used from UAL1_Standard.glb: Idle_Loop, Walk_Loop, Jog_Fwd_Loop,
  Jump_Start, Jump_Loop, Jump_Land, Roll, Idle_Talking_Loop.

- Universal Animation Library 2 [Standard]
  https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library-2
  Used from UAL2_Standard.glb: NinjaJump_Start, Idle_FoldArms_Loop, Yes.

The side and backward Walk_* / Jog_* clips are derived from Walk_Loop and
Jog_Fwd_Loop (turned about the vertical axis, and played backward for the
backward directions). Every clip is retargeted rotation-only onto the
character's skeleton.
